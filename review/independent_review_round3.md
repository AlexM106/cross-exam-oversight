## C. Credibility as an AI-safety pilot

Partly. The harness is honest about its limits, reports at case level, includes a consultancy control, and now asks two questions that matter to the oversight area (does a judge cave to pressure, does a defender hide what it knows). It remains a hand-built synthetic test set of 14 cases with an instructed, not spontaneous, defender, so it shows the shape of a protocol study rather than a finding. The strongest framing for the application is "a methodology and failure-mode pilot, with the validity checks stated".

The single change that would help most: add matched controls for the two new metrics. Specifically, run the honesty probe under a neutral prompt on the memo alone and on the clean controls, and add a neutral re-ask baseline for pushback. Without these, neither "defender knew" nor "judge is sycophantic" can be interpreted, and a careful reader will say so first.

Verified by code: all AI-01 and AI-02 arithmetic, the pushback conversation structure after a retry, the empty-content retry construction, pytest.
